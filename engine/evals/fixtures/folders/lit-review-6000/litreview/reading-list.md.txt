# Reading list (25)

1. McMahan et al. 2017 - *Communication-efficient learning of deep networks from decentralized data* - FedAvg; the baseline everyone cites [read]
2. Li et al. 2020 - *Federated optimization in heterogeneous networks* - FedProx; proximal term for non-IID clients [read]
3. Kairouz et al. 2021 - *Advances and open problems in federated learning* - survey; use for structure [read]
4. Zhao et al. 2018 - *Federated learning with non-IID data* - weight divergence under skew [read]
5. Karimireddy et al. 2020 - *SCAFFOLD* - control variates for client drift
6. Sheller et al. 2020 - *Federated learning in medicine* - multi-institution brain tumour segmentation [read]
7. Rieke et al. 2020 - *The future of digital health with federated learning* - position paper
8. Dayan et al. 2021 - *Federated learning for predicting clinical outcomes in COVID-19* - EXAM model, 20 sites
9. Kaissis et al. 2020 - *Secure, privacy-preserving and federated machine learning in medical imaging* - review
10. Bonawitz et al. 2017 - *Practical secure aggregation* - secure aggregation protocol [read]
11. Abadi et al. 2016 - *Deep learning with differential privacy* - DP-SGD
12. Geyer et al. 2017 - *Differentially private federated learning* - client-level DP
13. Zhu et al. 2019 - *Deep leakage from gradients* - gradient inversion attack
14. Hitaj et al. 2017 - *Deep models under the GAN* - information leakage attack
15. Wang et al. 2020 - *Federated learning with matched averaging* - FedMA
16. Hsu et al. 2019 - *Measuring the effects of non-identical data distribution* - Dirichlet label skew benchmark
17. Li et al. 2021 - *FedBN* - local batch norm for feature shift
18. Smith et al. 2017 - *Federated multi-task learning* - MOCHA
19. Fallah et al. 2020 - *Personalized federated learning with theoretical guarantees* - Per-FedAvg
20. T Dinh et al. 2020 - *Personalized federated learning with Moreau envelopes* - pFedMe
21. Roth et al. 2020 - *Federated learning for breast density classification* - real-world deployment
22. Guo et al. 2021 - *Multi-institutional collaborations for improving deep learning-based MRI reconstruction* - FL-MRCM
23. Liu et al. 2021 - *FedDG* - domain generalisation in medical segmentation
24. Yang et al. 2019 - *Federated machine learning: concept and applications* - taxonomy horizontal/vertical
25. Lyu et al. 2020 - *Threats to federated learning: a survey* - attacks overview
